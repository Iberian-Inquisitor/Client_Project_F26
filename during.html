During Your Experience

Manage Your Engaged Learning Experience

During an engaged learning experience, students manage projects, communicate with partners, respond to challenges, and reflect on their learning.

Key Skills During the Experience

Communicate with Partners

Share updates clearly, ask questions, listen carefully, and confirm that everyone understands the project goals.

Manage Projects and Teams

Track responsibilities, organize deadlines, and communicate with teammates when project needs change.

Respond to Challenges

Engaged learning projects may include unexpected problems. Use feedback, ethical decision-making, and collaborative problem-solving to move forward.

Reflect on Learning

Consider how the experience is changing your skills, perspective, understanding of the community, and approach to future projects.
